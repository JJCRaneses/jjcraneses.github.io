Undergraduate Dissertation
The AI Implementation Gap: Policy–Audit Misalignment in the UAE and Egypt
My dissertation examines how artificial intelligence is being adopted in the audit profession by comparing what Big Four firms publicly report with the experiences of their auditors in the United Arab Emirates and Egypt. Through an analysis of corporate transparency reports and practitioner interviews, the research reveals a gap between organizational claims and day-to-day practice, highlighting the continuing importance of human judgment, professional skepticism, and effective AI governance in external audit audit engagements.
